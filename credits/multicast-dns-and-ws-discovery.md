Section: Specifications and papers
- Multicast DNS and DNS-Based Service Discovery, from
  [RFC 6762](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc6762) and
  [RFC 6763](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc6763), by Stuart Cheshire
  and Marc Krochmal, which discoverd(8) answers and asks by, and which
  resolved(8) sends `.local` names to
  ([ADR 0338](docs/adr/0338-discoverd-finds-what-the-local-networks-have-with-mdns-and-ws-discovery.md)).
- Web Services Dynamic Discovery (WS-Discovery) of April 2005, by BEA
  Systems, Canon, Intel, Microsoft and webMethods, whose Probe discoverd
  sends and whose ProbeMatches it reads.
