# ALR-104: A browser ended its push subscription (removed)

A browser that gets alerts has a subscription at its maker's push service, and the service told alertd that it is gone: it answered 404 or 410 to a message. The person turned notifications off for this console in the browser, or cleared the site's data, or the browser dropped the subscription itself. alertd removed the subscription, logged it and put a `push-ended` line in the alerts' history, and sends nothing more to that browser. Nothing is wrong with the machine.

## What to do

1. If the browser should get alerts again, open the Alerts page in it and choose **Get alerts in this browser**. It asks for permission again and makes a new subscription.
2. If it should not, nothing needs doing: the subscription is out of Where alerts go.
3. `elevate alerts history` shows the `push-ended` line with the time.
