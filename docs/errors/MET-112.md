# MET-112: A disk behind a RAID controller failed or is expected to

A disk behind a RAID controller has failed, or the controller or the disk itself expects it to fail. metricsd raises an alert with the key `raid-disk:CONTROLLER-ID`, critical for a disk that failed and a warning for one that is expected to. The title names the disk by its bay and the controller. The alert is cleared when the disk is replaced, or when the disk is no longer expected to fail.

This is the same fact as the failure of `hardware check raid` (HW-172, HW-173), told when it happens and not when someone looks.

## What to do

1. Run `storage raid` to see the bay, and `diskhealth raid0:bay4` (with the controller's number and the bay in place of the example) to see what the disk says.
2. Back up what is on the volume, and replace the disk in its bay, as HW-172 and HW-173 say.
3. To check that it worked, run `storage raid` and see the disk, and the volume it was in, in good state.
