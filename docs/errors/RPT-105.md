# RPT-105: A line the system gathered can be cut, not changed

What the system gathered for a report, such as a crash's frames or a service's log, is sent as the system wrote it, so that the people who read it can trust it. A person may cut any of it, a whole line or a part of one, which then reads `[cut]`; a person may not type into it. Your own answers to the three questions can be typed again freely. The Report a problem page asked for a change that is neither: text typed into a gathered line, or a cut of a line, a section or characters the draft does not have, as when the page shows an older draft than reportd keeps. The message says where. Nothing was changed, and the draft is kept as it was.

## What to do

1. Reload the Report a problem page and make the cuts again on the draft it shows.
2. To add something to a gathered line, write it in your answer to **What happened** instead, where it is read with the rest.
3. If it happens again after a reload, report it from a terminal with `report`, which lets you change the whole text in your editor before it is sent.
