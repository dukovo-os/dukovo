# ADM-130: Too many terminals

The console holds as many terminals as it may. The detail says which limit was met:

- Each account keeps at most 32 terminals, or the number `sessions-per-account` sets in the `[terminal]` table of `/system/settings/admin.toml`, from 1 to 64.
- Each terminal holds at most four panes.
- The console holds at most 256 panes for everyone together.
- A browser tab attaches at most 16 panes at once, and a session opens at most four connections for terminals.

A terminal keeps running while no page shows it, so terminals left open count until they are ended or end by themselves. Nothing was opened.

## What to do

1. Open the Terminal's Sessions page and end the terminals you no longer use. Ending a terminal ends its shells.
2. For a pane, close one of the terminal's panes, or open a new terminal.
3. An administrator who needs more terminals for each account can raise `sessions-per-account` in `admin.toml`.
