# HW-141: A battery holds under 80% of its design capacity

A battery holds less charge when full as it ages. The check compares what the battery holds when full now with what it held when new, from the battery's own figures (`energy_full` and `energy_full_design`, or the charge pair, in `/kernel/class/power_supply`). Under 80% the battery is worn out: it runs the machine for under four fifths of the time it did, and the loss goes on faster from there. The line gives the percentage and, where the battery says, the number of charge cycles.

## What to do

1. Run `elevate hardware check battery` to see the figure again. Do it after a full charge, since a battery that has not been charged reads low.
2. Replace the battery with one made for the machine. A battery that swells, or a laptop whose case lifts or whose trackpad rises, must be taken out at once and not charged again.
3. Until then, the machine works as before on its power adapter; only the time on battery is shorter.
4. To check that it worked, run `elevate hardware check battery` with the new battery and see it pass.
