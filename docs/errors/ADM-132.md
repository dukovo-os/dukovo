# ADM-132: The terminal is not open to this account

The console's terminal is not open to this account, for one of two reasons, which the detail names:

- The `[terminal]` table of `/system/settings/admin.toml` sets `allowed` to `"administrators"` and this account is not one, or to `"nobody"`.
- The account has no shell to start. A service's account has none: its shell is `/system/commands/false`, which ends at once.

The rest of the console works as before. Nothing was opened.

## What to do

1. For `allowed`, ask an administrator: they set who may open a terminal in `admin.toml`, which is read again at each request for a terminal.
2. A service's account is for its service to run as, not for a person: sign in to the console with your own account.
