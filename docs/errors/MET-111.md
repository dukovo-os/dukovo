# MET-111: A RAID volume is degraded or offline

A volume of a RAID controller is degraded, partly degraded or offline. metricsd raises a critical alert, key `raid-volume:CONTROLLER-TARGET`, for a degraded or an offline volume, and a warning for a partly degraded one. The title names the volume and the controller. A degraded volume works but has lost some or all of its protection: another disk failing before it is whole again can lose its data. An offline volume cannot be used. The alert is cleared when the volume is optimal again.

This is the same fact as the failure of `hardware check raid` (HW-171), told when it happens and not when someone looks.

## What to do

1. Run `storage raid` to see which disk is missing or failed, and the rebuild if one runs.
2. Back up what matters on the volume, and follow HW-171: put a new disk in the failed disk's bay.
3. To check that it worked, wait for the rebuild to end. `storage raid` says the volume is working normally, and the alert is cleared.
