# ADM-118: This session may only add a second step

The sign-in rules, `second-step` in `/system/settings/admin.toml`, ask this account for a second step, and it has none. Its session may add one, and sign out, and do nothing else until it has: the Second step page is the only page it gets. This answer is for any other request, from a tab left open or from a script.

Once a second step is added, the page asks the person to sign in again, now with the step, and that session may do all that the account may.

## What to do

1. On the Second step page, add an authenticator app or a passkey. It asks for the password. Keep the recovery codes it shows once.
2. Sign in again.
3. If the rules should not ask this account for one, an administrator changes `second-step` in `/system/settings/admin.toml`; the account signs in again.
