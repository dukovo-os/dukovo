# ADM-107: This session has ended

The browser has no session, or the one it has is over, so the console does not know who is asking. A session ends when:

- the person signs out;
- it reaches its time limit, `session-hours` in admin.toml, 12 hours by default;
- no console page of it has been open for `idle-minutes`, 30 by default;
- its worker stops, because the account was disabled or may no longer use the console, or authd started again;
- admind stops or restarts, which signs everyone out.

A sign-in under way also ends: a second step has to come within 5 minutes of the password, from the same address.

The sign-in page shows why a session ended, under "Sign in again". An answer of this kind also tells the browser to forget its cookie.

## What to do

1. Sign in again.
2. If the session ends as soon as you sign in, check that the browser keeps cookies for the console. The cookie, `__Host-dukovo`, belongs to one name, so opening the console by its name and then by its address starts two different sessions.
3. If sessions end too soon or too late for the people who use them, an administrator changes `session-hours` and `idle-minutes` in `/system/settings/admin.toml`; they apply from the next check, within 30 seconds.
