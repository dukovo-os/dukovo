# HW-163: A PCIe device stopped after a fatal error

A PCIe device sent a fatal error, or the port above it contained one and reset the link. The kernel marked the device failed. Its driver cannot use it until the machine restarts, so a network card stops carrying traffic and a disk controller stops serving its disks. The line gives the device's PCI address and name.

The kernel's record of the error is in `/kernel/ras/errors`, and the device's own counts are in its folder under `/kernel/bus/pci/devices`.

## What to do

1. Run `hardware devices` to see the device marked `FAILED` and its error counts, and `hardware errors` to see what it reported.
2. Back up what matters now, in case the device was serving data.
3. Restart the machine. A device that failed once because of a glitch works again after the restart.
4. If it fails again, reseat the card, try another slot, and update its firmware. Check that the machine's cooling and power are sound.
5. Replace the device if it still fails.
6. To check that it worked, run `elevate hardware check errors` after a day of use. The record starts empty at each start.
