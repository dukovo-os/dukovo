# NET-178: A card that serves DHCP needs an address of its own

`network set` was asked to change a card whose section has a `dhcp-server` setting, in a way that would stop its DHCP server. A card gives the computers on its network their addresses only from an address of its own, on the network that the range of `dhcp-server` lies in. So it cannot ask DHCP for its address (`network set CARD dhcp`), and its new addresses (`network set CARD fixed …`) must keep one on the range's network. Nothing was changed.

## What to do

1. To keep serving DHCP, give the card an address on the network of its range: `elevate network set CARD fixed ADDRESS/PREFIX`, with an address of that network that the range does not hold. The words of the refusal name the range.
2. To have the card ask DHCP or move to another network instead, remove or change the `dhcp-server` lines of its section in /system/settings/network first, then run `network set` again. With them removed, the card stops giving addresses to the computers on its network.
