# ADM-106: The console is locked

A change that needs an administrator was asked while the console was locked. The console starts every session locked: it shows what the account may see, and runs nothing as an administrator until the person proves again that it is them. The header says *Limited access*. It locks again when its 15 minutes run out, when someone presses Lock, when the person signs out, and a minute after the last console page of the session closes. Pressing Reset to 15 min after the time ran out gets this message too.

## What to do

1. Press *Unlock* in the header, and prove it is you with your passkey, the code from your app, or your password.
2. Make the change again. The header shows the minutes left, and Reset to 15 min sets them back to 15.
3. An account that is not an administrator cannot unlock: it may look and not change. Ask an administrator.
