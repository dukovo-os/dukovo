# HW-111: A disk says it is failing

A disk's own record of its health, its SMART attributes for a SATA disk or the health log of an NVMe drive, says that it is about to fail. For a SATA disk, an attribute that warns of failure is at or below its threshold; for an NVMe drive, a critical warning says its spare capacity is short, its reliability is degraded, it went read-only or its backup power failed. The line names the disk and gives the reasons it gave. Disks that say so often fail within weeks, and some within hours.

## What to do

1. Back up what is on the disk now, before anything else, to another disk. Do not run a full check on it first: reading a failing disk end to end is hard on it. The files that matter most come first.
2. Run `diskhealth DISK` to see all of its attributes and the reasons in full.
3. Replace the disk. A disk in a Btrfs volume with a second copy of its data is replaced with `replace start`, which rebuilds it from the other disks; see replace(8).
4. To check that it worked, run `elevate hardware check disks` and see the new disk's health line say `PASSED`.
