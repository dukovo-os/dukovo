# RPT-104: These lines cannot be changed

The first lines of a report say where it came from: the version of Dukovo, the kind of computer, what the report is about, and how many lines its sender cut. The people who read reports need them to know what a report is about, and they hold nothing that names you, your computer or your network: reportd takes those out of every line before the report is shown. The Report a problem page asked reportd to cut one of these lines, or a heading, which the page does not offer, so the request did not come from the page as it is. Nothing was changed, and the draft is kept as it was.

## What to do

1. Reload the Report a problem page, read the draft again, and cut what you want from the lines below the headings.
2. If a line at the top says something you do not want sent, do not send the report: choose **Save to a file…**, change the file as you wish, and send it by other means.
3. If it happens again after a reload, report it from a terminal with `report`, which shows the text it sends.
