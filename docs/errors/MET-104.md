# MET-104: A disk of a Btrfs volume is behind

A disk of a Btrfs volume missed changes: it was away, or failed for a while, and came back after the others had gone on. The message names the volume and says how many of its disks are behind out of how many it has. The volume is read from the other disks first, and the disk that is behind has stale copies until a scrub brings them up to date, so the volume has less redundancy than it was made with.

## What to do

1. Run `scrub start` on a folder of the volume, as `scrub start /volumes/vda`. It reads every copy, checks it and writes the good copy over a stale or bad one, and its summary says what it mended.
2. If the same disk is behind again soon, it is failing. `diskhealth` shows what the disk says of its health, and `log -k` the errors the kernel saw from it. Replace it as MET-103 says if it is.
3. The warning is cleared when no disk of the volume is behind.
