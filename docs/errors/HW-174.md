# HW-174: A RAID controller's battery has failed or is worn

The controller keeps writes in its cache before they reach the disks, and its battery (or capacitor module, a cache vault) keeps the cache until the power is back. The line says the battery has failed or is missing, which fails the check, or that it is worn, or its last test of its own capacity failed or ran out of time, which is a warning. While the battery cannot hold the cache, a volume set to write back writes straight to the disks, so that nothing is lost in a power cut; `storage raid` says so under the battery. The volume is slower for it, most of all for many small writes.

## What to do

1. Run `storage raid` to see the battery's state, charge and temperature. A battery that is charging or testing itself is no fault: it is not reported until the state is failed, missing or worn.
2. Replace the battery or the cache vault with one made for the controller. The module is usually on the controller's board, or on a cable from it.
3. Until then, nothing is lost: the writes go straight to the disks.
4. To check that it worked, run `elevate hardware check raid` with the new battery and see it pass once it has charged.
