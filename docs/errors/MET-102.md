# MET-102: A filesystem is filling fast

metricsd draws a line through the use of a filesystem over the last 6 hours, and warns when the filesystem is half full or more and the line reaches its size within 3 days, or the days that `filling-days` in metrics.toml says. The message says in about how long. The line needs 30 readings at least a minute apart, which metricsd takes from its history when it starts. The warning is cleared when the line reaches the size in more than one and a half times the days, or when the filesystem is under half full.

This is a rate, not a level: a filesystem that is 60 percent full and has grown by a few percent in the last hours gets this warning before it gets MET-101.

## What to do

1. Find out what is writing. `metrics show fs.used --since 6h --every 15m` shows how the use grew, and `top` shows what is running now. A program that writes a file or a log without end, a backup and a download are the usual causes.
2. Stop it or move it to another disk, and delete what it wrote that is not needed.
3. If the growth is expected, free space or add a disk before the day the message gives.

If the rate was a single burst that has ended, the warning clears by itself as the 6 hours slide on.
