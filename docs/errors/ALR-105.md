# ALR-105: The push service refused the message

alertd sent an alert, encrypted for a browser, to the endpoint of its subscription, and the browser maker's push service answered with something other than that it took the message. alertd does not follow a redirect and treats a 3xx as a refusal. A 404 or 410 is not this: that is a subscription that ended (ALR-104), and a 413, which says the message is too long, is sent again with a shorter detail. Any other answer is a failure: the line says the status and the service's words, and the message waits in the outbox, tried again after 1, 5, 15 and 30 minutes and then every hour, until it is 24 hours old.

## What to do

1. Read the status. 5xx and 429 are the push service being down or busy: nothing needs doing, alertd tries again. 400, 401 and 403 are the service refusing the message or the subscription, and trying again does not usually change them.
2. If it keeps refusing, make the subscription again: on the Alerts page in that browser, choose **Turn off in this browser**, then **Get alerts in this browser**. If a browser shows more than one subscription, remove the old ones under Where alerts go.
3. Check that this machine reaches the push service: `elevate alerts test` sends a test to every channel, push included, and says how each went.
4. To check that it worked, the test reaches the browser, and `elevate alerts status` shows no failure for it.
