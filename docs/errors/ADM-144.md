# ADM-144: This AI client may not do that

An AI client asked for something that it may not do. The detail says which of these it is:

- The action is in an area the client was not given. A client reads the areas chosen when it was added and no others.
- The action is a read that only administrators have, such as the whole log, and the client was given the area at the level Read, which is what its owner's account sees before it unlocks. Read all gives an area's every read.
- The client asked for a change in an area where it may read and not ask.
- The action is one no AI client is offered, whatever it was given: one that takes or shows a password, a key or a token; one that takes a document, or a read so long that it runs as a task; one that changes who gets in (the accounts, the sign-in rules, SSH, the private network, and the console's own settings, which hold the AI clients); or a change to what is a person's own, their account or their files, such as a new password or bringing back a file of theirs from a snapshot.
- Something tried to run a command with an AI client's token. A client changes nothing by itself: it asks, and an administrator approves on the AI clients page.

Nothing was read or changed.

## What to do

1. Read the detail: it names the action or the area.
2. To let a client reach more, remove it and add it again with the areas it needs, on the AI clients page or with `admin mcp add`. A client cannot be changed once it is added, so that what it may reach is always what an administrator saw when adding it.
3. For what no AI client is offered, do it yourself, on its page of the console or at a terminal.
