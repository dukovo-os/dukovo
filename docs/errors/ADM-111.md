# ADM-111: authd did not answer

The console's server asks authd for every step of a sign-in, and could not get an answer. authd was not there to connect to, closed the connection without answering, wrote an answer that does not read, or did not answer in 90 seconds, which is longer than the wait it puts on a wrong password. The detail says which, such as "cannot ask authd at /system/live/authd/socket" or "authd did not answer in 90 seconds".

authd may be starting, or busy for a second or two with another person's password. If it started again, every console session ended with it, because a session's worker is authd's child.

## What to do

1. Try again in a moment.
2. Check that authd runs: `elevate service authd status`.
3. Read what the two services said: `elevate log -s authd` and `elevate log -s admind`.
4. After authd started again, sign in again.

## See also

authd(8), admind(8), service(8), log(1), the handbook's chapter on the web console.
