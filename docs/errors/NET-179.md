# NET-179: Nothing is being tried

`network keep`, `network undo`, `firewall keep` or `firewall undo` was run, and Spark has no trial for that file. The change was kept or undone already, by this person or another, or its time ran out and Spark undid it, or it was never tried: a change made without `--try` is in force at once and has nothing to keep. Nothing was changed.

## What to do

1. Run `service list --json` to see the trials Spark keeps and the last ones it undid. The Networking and Firewall pages show the same, and the list of changes has a row for each undo.
2. If the change was undone, make it again, with a time to try it for.
