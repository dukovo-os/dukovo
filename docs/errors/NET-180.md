# NET-180: No rule has those words

`firewall remove` or `firewall replace` was given the words of a rule, and no rule of /system/settings/firewall is equal to it. The words are read as a rule, so their order does not matter (`allow 22 tcp` is `allow tcp 22`), but every part must be the same: `allow tcp 22` is not the rule `allow tcp 22 from 192.168.1.0/24`. Nothing was changed.

## What to do

1. Run `firewall list` to see the rules as the file holds them, with their numbers.
2. Run the command again with the words of one of them, or `firewall remove NUMBER` to remove a rule by its number.

## See also

firewall(8), the handbook's chapter on the web console.
