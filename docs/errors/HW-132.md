# HW-132: A fan stopped, or turns too slowly

A hardware monitor reports a fan that reads 0 RPM where it gives a minimum, that turns under its minimum, or whose alarm or fault is set. A stopped fan means the machine heats up as soon as it works. A fan that reads 0 RPM and gives no minimum is not judged, since the motherboard's headers with no fan on them read so.

## What to do

1. Run `sensors` to see which fan, and its speed against its minimum.
2. Check that the fan is plugged into its header, and that nothing, such as a cable, blocks it. Turn it by hand with the machine off; it must spin freely.
3. Clean it. A fan clogged with dust turns slowly. Replace a fan that makes noise, sticks or does not start.
4. If the fan turns and its reading is 0 RPM, it is plugged into a header that does not report its speed, or its sensor is wrong. The fan's own speed setting may be low; some machines stop a fan at low temperatures, which is no fault when its minimum is not set.
5. To check that it worked, run `elevate hardware check sensors` and see the fan line pass.
