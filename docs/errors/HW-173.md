# HW-173: A disk behind a RAID controller is predicted to fail

The disk still works, but the controller, or the disk's own warning (SMART), says it is expected to fail: the controller counts a predictive failure, or the disk raised its alert. The line names the disk by its bay and gives its model and serial number. Disks that say so often fail within weeks. Until one does, the volume has all its protection; after it does, the volume is degraded (HW-171).

## What to do

1. Back up what is on the volume now, to another disk.
2. Run `diskhealth raid0:bay4`, with the controller's number and the disk's bay in place of the example, to see what the disk itself says. `storage raid` lists the bays.
3. Replace the disk in its bay before it fails, with one at least as large. The controller rebuilds the volume on the new disk.
4. To check that it worked, run `elevate hardware check raid` and see no disk reported.
