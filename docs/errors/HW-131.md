# HW-131: A temperature is over its limit at rest

A hardware monitor reports a temperature, read before the load of the check began, that is at its critical limit (a failure) or over its high limit (a warning), or whose own alarm is set. The line names the sensor, its temperature and the limit. A machine at rest should be well under its limits, so a high reading means that heat is not leaving the machine, that a fan is not working, or that a sensor is wrong.

## What to do

1. Run `sensors` to see every temperature and fan with their limits, and see which one is high.
2. Check that the fans turn (see HW-132), the vents are clear of dust, and the cooler is seated. Move a laptop off any soft surface.
3. If the same sensor reads very high or very low all the time and nothing is hot to the touch, the sensor may be wrong, a thing some firmware does; check that its reading changes when the machine works.
4. To check that it worked, run `elevate hardware check sensors` after the machine has cooled, and see the temperature line pass.
