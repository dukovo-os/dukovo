# ADM-120: The console is full

The console holds as many signed-in sessions as it may, 64, and cannot start another. The new session is ended again, so that none is half made. authd keeps the same count, and when it is the one that says so the detail is in its words: "64 web console sessions are signed in already".

This is about sessions. A connection that arrives when the console has 512 connections, or 32 from its address, is closed with no answer, and the browser shows a connection error.

## What to do

1. Try again when someone has signed out.
2. Sign out of sessions that are not in use: each browser that signed in has one. An unused session ends by itself after `idle-minutes` with no page open, 30 by default, and after `session-hours`, 12 by default.
3. An administrator who finds the console full often can lower both in `/system/settings/admin.toml`.
