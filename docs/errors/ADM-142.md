# ADM-142: A trial runs: keep or undo it before a restart

A restart or a shut down of the machine, or a search with `changes bisect`, which restarts it, was asked for while Spark lists a trial: a change of settings, such as the network's, the firewall's, SSH's, who may reach the console, the console's certificate or a service's file, that is in force for a while and is undone unless it is kept. A restart in the middle of a trial ends it half-way: Spark puts the old settings back at the next start whatever was meant, and nobody would know why. So the console refuses first. It refuses the same when it cannot tell whether a trial runs, because Spark did not answer: it does not let a restart through on a guess. Nothing was restarted.

## What to do

1. Look at the header: the chip for a trial names the page that owns it and the time left. On that page, choose **Keep this change** if it works, or **Undo now** if not.
2. At a terminal, `service list --json` prints the trials Spark keeps, with the time each has left. `elevate network keep` or `elevate network undo`, `elevate firewall keep` or `elevate firewall undo`, and `elevate admin keep` or `elevate admin undo` end the ones they name.
3. Or wait: Spark undoes a trial when its time runs out.
4. Ask for the restart again. If the words say that the console cannot tell whether a trial runs, run `service list`: it shows whether Spark answers.
