# HW-142: A UPS says its battery should be replaced

A UPS tests its own battery, and says when the battery no longer holds the charge it should (the Need Replacement flag of its USB reports, which the kernel shows as `health` `Dead` in `/kernel/class/power_supply/ups0`). A UPS battery lasts three to five years. Worn out, it may not carry the machine through a power cut long enough for the clean shutdown, or at all.

## What to do

1. Run `hardware power` to see the UPS and what it says of its battery now.
2. Replace the battery with the one the UPS's maker names for the model. Most UPSes take a new battery without being turned off; follow the maker's steps.
3. Many UPSes say this until they have run a self-test on the new battery: start one from the UPS's own buttons, or wait for the next one it runs.
4. To check that it worked, run `elevate hardware check battery` and see the UPS's line pass.
