# ADM-127: The console has no such change or task

The change or the task asked for is not one the console keeps: its number is not one it gave, or it is older than it keeps. The journal keeps the newest 10,000 changes, and a task is kept for 30 days after it ends.

## What to do

1. Reload the page: its list may be older than the console's.
2. A change that is gone is not coming back. A file's earlier versions are kept for its last 20 versions, so the files themselves, and the log of admind, are what is left to read.
