# ADM-134: The console follows as many logs as it may

The Logs page follows new lines as they come, which takes one long-running connection and one command for as long as it lasts. The console runs eight follows at once for everyone together, and this one would have been the ninth. Nothing was started. The answer says how many seconds to wait before asking again.

A session follows one log at a time: a follow begun in a second tab ends the first, which is not an error. A follow ends by itself after an hour, when the person locks the console or signs out, and when its tab is closed or has been hidden for a minute.

## What to do

1. Wait the seconds the answer names, then press **Follow again**.
2. Close the tabs of Logs that are following, or switch their **Follow new lines** off: each follow they hold counts until it ends.
3. To follow the log without the console, run `elevate log --follow` at a terminal.
