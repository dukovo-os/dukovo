# HW-171: A RAID volume is degraded or offline

A volume of a RAID controller has lost a disk. The line names the controller and the volume, its level and size, and its state: *degraded* when a disk is missing or has failed, *partly degraded* when only some of the volume's parts have, and *offline* when the volume cannot be used at all. When a disk is rebuilding, the line says which bay and how far it is. A degraded volume still works, from the disks it has, but it has lost some or all of its protection, and a second failure before it is whole again can lose its data. An offline volume cannot be read or written.

The controller's own answers are in `/kernel/raid`, and anyone may read them.

## What to do

1. Run `storage raid` to see which disk is missing or failed, by its bay, and whether a rebuild is running and how long it has left.
2. Back up what matters on the volume now, while it still works. Do not wait for the rebuild.
3. Replace the failed disk in its bay with one at least as large. The controller starts the rebuild by itself when a hot spare is set, or when the new disk is put in the bay.
4. Let the rebuild end before anything else is changed. `storage raid` shows its progress, and the volume is optimal when it is done.
5. To check that it worked, run `elevate hardware check raid` and see the volume pass.
