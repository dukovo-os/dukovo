# ADM-136: Not on your own account from the console

The request would end the account you are signed in with, or the session you are using: disabling or removing your own account, taking the administrator right from it, or ending the session this browser is using. The console refuses these before anything runs, for a session and for an API token alike, because each would cut you off from the console in the middle of what you asked for, and nothing could undo it from here. Nothing was changed.

## What to do

1. For your own account, ask another administrator to do it from their account. `user` refuses it at a terminal too, since the session it runs in would end under it.
2. To leave this session, use **Sign out**, which ends it as you intend. To end another of your sessions, open Your account and choose **End** beside it.
3. The machine also keeps one enabled administrator whatever is asked: ACC-102 is that rule, and it holds at a terminal as well.
