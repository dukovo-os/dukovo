# MET-106: A sensor is close to its limit

A temperature sensor has been within 5 degrees Celsius of its critical temperature, or the margin that `temperature-margin` in metrics.toml says, for 5 readings in a row; a sensor with no critical temperature gives the warning at its maximum. The message names the sensor, as the chip and its label, and says its temperature and its limit. The warning is critical at the critical temperature itself. It is cleared when the sensor is 3 degrees below the line it was raised at.

A processor or a disk that gets too hot may slow itself down, and at its critical temperature the machine may stop to protect it.

## What to do

1. Run `sensors` to see every sensor, and `top` to see whether something is using all of the processors.
2. Check that the fans turn, that the vents are not covered and that dust has not filled them, and that the room is not hot.
3. If the machine was moved or its case opened, check that the cooler is seated.
4. Check that it worked: `metrics show temp --since 1h` shows the temperatures falling.
