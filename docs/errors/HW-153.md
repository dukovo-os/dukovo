# HW-153: A network card counted receive errors during the check

The card counted received frames that were damaged on the way, with a bad checksum or a wrong length, while the check ran: the count at the end is higher than at the start. A few are normal on a busy network. Many, or a count that rises on a quiet one, mean that the wire is damaged, that it is too long or near a source of noise, or that a port or the card is failing.

## What to do

1. Run `nic CARD --statistics` to see the counts of the card, and run it again a minute later to see which rise.
2. Replace the cable, and plug it into another port of the switch. A cable that is bent sharply, pinched by a door or run beside a power cable gives these errors.
3. If a second cable and port still give them, try the card in another machine, or another card in this one.
4. To check that it worked, run `elevate hardware check network` and see no receive errors during the check.
