# HW-114: A disk was very slow, or a read failed and then worked

The read scan of `hardware check` found reads that took over 2 seconds for 4 MiB, which is a speed of under 2 MiB a second, or reads that failed and worked when it tried again in halves. No sector was lost. A slow or flaky read comes from a disk that is starting to fail, which keeps trying a sector before it gives it up, or from a cable, a port or a power supply that does not carry the signal well.

## What to do

1. Back up what is on the disk, to another disk. This is early, but a disk that reads slowly is often the first sign.
2. Check the cable and the port. Push both ends in, try another cable, and try another port. A SATA cable that is bent sharply or pulled loose gives this, as does a USB enclosure on a weak power supply.
3. Run the check again. If the slow reads go with the disk to another cable and port, the disk is the fault: replace it. If they stay at the port, the port or the cable is.
4. To check that it worked, run `elevate hardware check disks`: the read scan must say `none unreadable, none slow`.
