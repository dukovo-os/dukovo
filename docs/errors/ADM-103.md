# ADM-103: The passkey was not accepted

authd checked a passkey that the browser gave, to sign in, to unlock the console or to add it, and did not take it. The detail holds authd's words and says why:

- The passkey is not one of this account's for the name that the console was opened by. A passkey belongs to one name, the one the console was opened by when it was added, such as `duke-nas.local`. A browser refuses a passkey for an address, so none can be made or used when the console is opened by one.
- The challenge is not one authd gave, was used already, or is more than 5 minutes old. Each try asks for a new one.
- The passkey did not check that it is you. To sign in with a passkey alone, and to unlock, the key must ask for its PIN, fingerprint or face.
- The signature is not the key's, or the page it was used on is not the console's.
- The passkey says no one touched it. The key has to be touched, or answered with its PIN, fingerprint or face.
- The key's count did not rise. A key that counts its uses gives a higher number each time, so one that does not may have been copied, and it is not taken. A key that never counts, and always gives 0, is taken.
- It is a kind of key the console does not check. It takes ES256, EdDSA and RS256 keys.
- The passkey is added already, or the account has 16 and has to remove one first.

## What to do

1. Read the detail under the title: it says which.
2. Try again, and follow the browser's prompt. Touch the key, and give its PIN or fingerprint when it asks.
3. Open the console by the name the passkey was added at. Second step lists each passkey with its name.
4. Sign in another way meanwhile: the password and an app code, or a recovery code.
5. If the count did not rise, remove the passkey from Second step and add a new one.
