# Trademark policy

> **Draft.** This policy has not yet been reviewed by a lawyer. It will be
> reviewed, and may change, before Dukovo's first public release.

Dukovo's code is free: anyone may use, study, change and share it under
Apache-2.0 or MIT. Its name is a different matter. The name tells people
where a system came from and who stands behind it, so that someone who
installs "Dukovo" gets the system this project builds, tests and signs,
and not something else under its name. This policy says how the name may
be used, and it aims to allow every use that does not mislead.

The licenses give no right to the name: Apache-2.0 says so in its
section 6, and the MIT license grants rights in the software alone.

## The marks

- **Dukovo**, the word, in any style or typeface. Dukovo™ is a
  trademark (U.S. trademark application serial no. 50194034).
- **Dukovo's logo** and **Duke**, its mascot, as in
  `docs/images/dukovo-logo-*.svg`, and any later logo. The artwork's
  files are under the terms of `LICENSES/LicenseRef-Dukovo-Marks.txt`,
  which this policy fills in.

The names of Dukovo's programs and services, such as `consoled` or
`nukesh`, are not marks and may be used freely.

## Uses that need no permission

- **Saying what is true.** Writing about Dukovo, reviewing it, teaching
  it, comparing it with other systems, and naming it in talks, books,
  videos and articles.
- **Sharing Dukovo unchanged.** Passing on the images the project
  publishes, unaltered, under their name, for free or for a fee; and
  building a release tag from its unmodified source and sharing the
  result under the name.
- **Packaging.** Packaging Dukovo's programs for another system under
  their names, with only the changes packaging needs, as long as the
  package says who made the changes.
- **Saying what works with Dukovo.** "Runs on Dukovo", "for Dukovo" and
  "compatible with Dukovo", for software or hardware that does, as long
  as the product's own name is not Dukovo or close to it.
- **Community.** User groups, meetups, forums, chat rooms and
  non-commercial events about Dukovo may use the name, such as "Dukovo
  users of Lisbon", with a plain statement that they are not run by the
  project.
- **Your own build.** Building Dukovo from source, changed or not, and
  running it on your own machines or your organisation's under its name.

## Modified versions

A system made from changed Dukovo source, shared with others, is a
different system, and needs a different name. It may say that it is
"based on Dukovo" or "derived from Dukovo" in its description, but its
name, its logo and the name it gives itself must not be Dukovo's or
confusingly close to them. Before sharing it, replace the name and the
logo everywhere the system shows them: the boot menu, the startup
screen, the installer, the system's release file, and the documentation.

Changes that packaging needs, and fixes taken unchanged from Dukovo's
own repository, do not make a modified version.

## Uses that need permission

- A product, service or company whose name includes Dukovo, or a name
  close to it, such as "Dukovo Cloud" or "Dukovify".
- A domain name or social media account that includes the name, other
  than for the community uses above.
- Merchandise for sale, such as shirts and stickers.
- The logo in another product's branding.
- Any claim that something is certified, approved or supported by the
  project.

Ask the maintainer, at the address in [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md).
Permission is given in writing, and the project means to give it
readily where a use does not mislead.

## Never allowed

- Suggesting that the project endorses, sponsors or is part of a
  product, company or cause when it does not.
- Using the name for software that is not Dukovo, or for a modified
  version, as above.
- Registering Dukovo, or a name close to it, as a trademark, company
  name or domain name of one's own.
- Using the name or logo in a way that misleads people about where
  something came from.

## Writing the name

Write Dukovo as one word with a capital D, and do not abbreviate it,
change its spelling or join it to other words. Where a product of the
project's is first named prominently, it may carry ™ until the mark is
registered, and ® after.

## Changes to this policy

The maintainer may change this policy in a pull request, as
[GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md) describes. A use that was allowed when it
began stays allowed under the policy of that time for a year after a
change that would forbid it.
